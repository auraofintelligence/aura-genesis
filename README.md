A single-page concept site for the Aura Genesis journey: a proposed sixty-session reflection, health, digital-twin and community experience framed as an open-ended “infinite game”.

## Public page

[Explore the live Aura Genesis concept](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis/).

## What the page covers

- a staged player journey;
- the proposed Aura Geode and personal digital-twin engine;
- connections to community, creative and planetary projects;
- a protopian “infinite game” framing; and
- a registration interface for different visitor interests.

The registration form is demonstration-only. Submitting it writes the entered form data to the browser console, hides the form and shows a thank-you message. It does not register a person, send an email or store an enquiry in a backend.

Open `index.html` in a browser for a local view. The page has no build step, but uses externally hosted fonts and some remote images.

## Status and health boundary

This is a vision prototype, not an operating program, medical service, approved device, clinical protocol or working digital twin. References to HBOT, biometrics, health outcomes and lifestyle change are concept material. They require current evidence, qualified clinical leadership, regulation, informed consent, privacy design and individual medical review before any real use.
